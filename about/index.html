A practical perspective.

Mohsin Khan’s approach centres on organised bookkeeping, clear reporting and helping business owners understand their figures.

Moxin Sterling Advisory supports sole traders, contractors, start-ups and small limited companies. We begin with the business in front of us: how it works, what records it keeps and where support would be useful.

For businesses with customers or suppliers beyond the UK, that conversation can also include how overseas transactions are recorded and which questions need separate specialist advice.

Our aim is a straightforward working relationship, with clear responsibilities and an agreed scope.
