A few useful answers

Before we begin.

Have another question?
We would be happy to discuss it.

How much will it cost?

Fees depend on your business, the condition of your records and the support you need. Request a tailored quote. We will agree the scope and fee before work begins.

Can you help if I am just starting out?

Yes. We can discuss the records you will need, the support that may be useful and a sensible starting point for your business.

Can I move from another accountant?

We can discuss what you need and the information needed for a handover. Any work and communication with your previous adviser will be agreed with you first.

Do I need to send sensitive documents with my enquiry?

No. Please do not send tax identifiers, bank details, passwords or identity documents through the enquiry form or initial email. A suitable way to share information can be agreed later.

What happens after I get in touch?

We will discuss your circumstances and the support you are looking for. You can then decide whether to proceed with an agreed scope and quote. An enquiry does not create an accountant-client relationship.
