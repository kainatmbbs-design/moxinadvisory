Website information

Website privacy.

This page describes the website’s email-draft enquiry mode. It does not cover the My Money app.

Last updated: 1 November 2026.

Who to contact

For questions about website enquiries, contact Moxin Sterling Advisory at contact@moxinadvisory.com.

The enquiry form

In the current email-draft mode, the website does not send form entries to a form service or store them in a website database. The form prepares a draft in your email app containing your name, email address, optional business name, business type, service of interest and message. You decide whether to send it.

If you send that email, your email provider and the recipient’s email provider process it. The recipient address is contact@moxinadvisory.com. Your message is used to read and respond to your enquiry and discuss any services you request. Please send only the information needed for that conversation.

Visiting the website

The website does not include analytics, advertising trackers, embedded maps or chat widgets. It does not set application cookies or use browser storage. The static hosting provider may process connection information such as IP addresses and request logs when delivering pages. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages; hosting information is available in the GitHub Privacy Statement at docs.github.com.

WhatsApp

The WhatsApp links open an external chat with our business number. No chat widget is embedded on this website. If you choose to message us, WhatsApp processes the conversation under its own privacy terms. Please keep initial enquiries brief and do not send sensitive financial documents.

Enquiries and your choices

Please do not include tax identifiers, bank details, passwords or identity documents in an initial enquiry. Contact us if you want to ask about access to, correction of or deletion of correspondence. Rights and any applicable exceptions depend on the relevant data protection law. You may also contact the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Before using professional services

An enquiry does not create an accountant-client relationship. Data handling for an engagement will need to be explained separately with the agreed work.

Publication status

The controller’s legal details, the lawful basis for handling enquiries, the retention period, hosting provider and any international transfer arrangements are awaiting confirmation. This page must be reviewed with those details before public launch. If a submission provider is enabled, this notice must be updated before it receives enquiries.

The app has separate data handling information. See My Money app privacy.
